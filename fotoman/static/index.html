      About

      Your local photo library — find what you kept, cull near-duplicates, and stay organized without the cloud.

      Version …

      Tips

      • Describe a scene in the search bar to find photos by meaning.
      • Browse the tree by Library, Collections, Dates, Places, or People.
      • Use Active / Rejected / All to focus on what you kept or soft-rejected.
      • Compare shows similar clusters; partners outside the tree selection are marked OUTSIDE.
      • Drag the timeline to select photos by date; right-click clears the brush selection.
      • Right-click a photo (or a selection) to edit dates and places. Build Collections without moving files; nest them, or export a collection as a folder tree (optionally a new volume).
      • Add volumes for phone dumps, NAS, or folders from Reclaimer exports.

      Preferences

      Gallery

      Off shows the full image (long edge). On crops to fill (short edge).

      Date edits

      On by default. Does not change file bytes or hash. Collection export also stamps dest files this way.

      Collections

      Also adds near-duplicates of the selected photos. Export writes current members only.

      Fotomob

      Collections are on Fotomob. Right-click a collection for the share link. Delete a collection to remove it.
